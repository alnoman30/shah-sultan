My story

My SEO journey started a decade ago, almost by accident. I was working on a small project for a friend’s business website — a site that looked good but couldn’t get a single visitor from Google. After weeks of digging into search engine guidelines, blog posts, and forums, I made a few changes. Within two months, the site was getting steady organic traffic. That moment changed everything for me.

I dove headfirst into SEO. Took every certification I could find, practiced on every project I could land, and learned by doing. The early years were intense — late nights understanding how Google really worked, which technical fixes moved the needle, and which content strategies compounded over time. Then AI search arrived and changed everything I thought I knew about organic traffic.

In 2018, I joined Upwork to expand my reach globally. That decision changed my career. Through Upwork’s transparent review system, every project became a chance to build verifiable credibility. I started with small audits, but my methodical approach — focused on results, transparent communication, and white-hat tactics — built me a 5-star reputation quickly.

Today I specialize in Full SEO and AI SEO together — because organic traffic now comes from Google, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, AI Overviews, and Answer Engines all at once. I’m a Top Rated Plus freelancer on Upwork with 226 projects, 5,708 hours, and $80k+ in earnings. Every number represents a real client whose organic traffic I helped grow.

What keeps me going isn’t the rankings or the traffic graphs. It’s the moments when a client emails saying "we just hired two more people because the inquiries won't stop" or "we hit our best month ever." SEO done right transforms businesses. That’s why I keep showing up every single day.
